## Primary guidance

- GOV.UK: [user needs](https://guidance.publishing.service.gov.uk/writing-to-gov-uk-standards/plan-manage-content/identify-user-needs/),
  [content structure](https://guidance.publishing.service.gov.uk/writing-to-gov-uk-standards/writing-guidelines/clear-structure/),
  [clear language](https://guidance.publishing.service.gov.uk/writing-to-gov-uk-standards/writing-guidelines/clear-language/),
  [button wording and emphasis](https://design-system.service.gov.uk/components/button/).
  Adapt user-task grounding, clear labels and nearby answers. Its government/
  English conventions (for example sentence limits or heading style) are not
  universal Russian copy rules or a compulsory commercial page structure.
- Nielsen Norman Group: [visual design principles](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/principles-visual-design/),
  [task success measurement](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/success-rate-the-simplest-usability-metric/),
  [satisfaction versus performance](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/satisfaction-vs-performance-metrics/).
  Adopt evidence-based hierarchy diagnosis and distinguish task completion from
  preference. Task-success categories are reported separately rather than
  averaged with arbitrary partial-credit weights. Typography suggestions and
  old study proportions are not universal gates.
- W3C WCAG 2.2: [normative criteria](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/),
  [headings and labels](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/headings-and-labels.html),
  [text contrast](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/contrast-minimum.html),
  [text-spacing adaptation](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/text-spacing.html).
  Distinguish scoped measurable accessibility checks from general comprehension
  and full conformance. Spacing-override requirements do not dictate default CSS.

The research also found MeasuringU's first-click replication. Its search result
