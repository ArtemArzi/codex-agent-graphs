# Measure the question, not an invented clarity score

Choose evidence proportionate to the requested decision. Not every text edit
needs a user study or production experiment. Keep expert review, formative human
testing and observed production outcomes separate. No minimum sample, time limit
or percentage is a universal acceptance standard.

## Before a test

Define the audience/task, version/state/device, critical facts to understand,
acceptable answer meanings and successful action/outcome before observing
results. Do not redefine success after seeing failures. For comprehension, name
the essential meaning rather than require exact wording. Record participant
selection and prior familiarity; colleagues and the author may know hidden facts.

Preserve a comparable baseline if evaluating change. Choose a small feasible
study for discovering problems, or a properly planned larger comparison for
population/performance claims. Low traffic and small samples can still reveal
issues; they cannot establish a stable conversion uplift.

## Select a metric with its interpretation

| Question | Observation/metric | Evidence limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Is the needed answer present and findable in the design? | Expert criterion record with element/evidence: observed, concern, not checked, not applicable | Presence is not human comprehension; avoid a weighted /100 score |
| What did people understand? | Count of participants whose unprompted explanation preserves each predefined essential fact, out of those actually tested | Code each fact separately; a model's persona walkthrough is a hypothesis, not participant data |
| Can they locate an answer? | Found/not found/assisted; elapsed time to the defined answer point, with timeout/abandonment tracked | Explain the time boundary; show failed and assisted attempts separately rather than timing only successes |
| Is the next action/consequence clear? | First chosen action and the participant's description of what will happen | Several first paths may be valid; a screenshot click is not completion of the actual workflow |
| Can they complete the task? | Unassisted success n/N, plus assisted, partial, failed and abandoned outcomes separately | Define success beforehand. Do not average arbitrary partial-credit levels into a success rate |
| Does it feel easy? | A clearly described post-task ease question with the scale and responses | Self-report is different from task success and understanding; no custom scale becomes a validated instrument by naming it |
| What happened on the live site? | Existing action-to-completion funnel and reconciled outcome with explicit denominator/period | CTR, scroll and dwell time alone do not establish understanding, causality or business value |

Use counts such as 3/5 and the actual mistakes for a small formative study.
Always disclose the denominator, exclusions, task and version. Separate missing
observations from observed failures. An abandoned started task belongs among
reported outcomes; do not silently discard it to improve the success rate.
For broader statistical claims use an appropriate design, uncertainty estimates
and sampling assumptions; five participants do not guarantee a representative
percentage. Do not pool different tasks/devices/versions without showing the mix.

## Ask without teaching the answer

Give a realistic task without naming the intended control. Let an unfamiliar
participant describe the offer/state, relevant conditions and expected next
step in their own words. A useful opening inspired by the supplied practitioner
post asks what stands out, how the person interprets it and what they would do.
Do not explain the screen first, ask only "is everything clear?", or praise and
correct answers while collecting a baseline. Record actual words and behaviour.

Brief exposure can test first impression or recall if duration and questions
are specified. It does not prove full comprehension of price conditions or task
completion. Think-aloud can reveal reasons but affects timing; do not mix its
durations with silent task timings as equivalent measurements.

Use human participants or supplied real observations for human findings. Agent
simulation, code review and screenshot critique can find candidate issues but
cannot generate human success counts, gaze locations or conversion estimates.

## Interpret and act

Treat a critical false belief (for example, expecting a booking when only a
request is sent) as a specific defect to resolve even if other criteria look
good. Tie a proposed rewrite or placement change to that observed problem and
retest the affected question/flow. Preserve factual/legal meaning and the
product's actual promise.

For before/after results keep task, audience, device and conditions comparable;
record learning/order effects, traffic-source changes and concurrent releases.
An A/B test needs a valid design, exposure/outcome definitions, guardrails and
adequate data for its stated claim. Do not prescribe an arbitrary seven-day run,
ten-percent uplift or universal p-value target to every small site. A corrected
message or improved small-study result is not automatically increased sales.

When there are no participants or analytics, deliver the supported expert
findings and a short runnable check with predefined questions/success criteria.
Label human and production metrics not measured. A completed skill validation
or synthetic scenario does not change that status.
