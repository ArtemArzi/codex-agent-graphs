# Evidence and repeatable comparisons

Use the existing project report/evidence owner. The goal is a useful diagnosis,
not a fixed number of prompts, a new monitoring service or an artificial score.
Choose a bounded sample appropriate to the question and available authority.

## Choose questions that test the intended result

Use real customer questions and confirmed business facts. Distinguish:

- Branded discovery: can the system find and describe the named organisation?
- Non-branded choice: does it select or cite the business without being given
  its name/domain, for a relevant service, location or problem?
- Known-URL reading: can it retrieve specific current facts from a supplied URL?
- Comparison or explanation: are the supported differences and conditions
  represented correctly, with sources?

Do not count a supplied-domain reading task as spontaneous discovery. Topics
inferred from interviews are hypotheses until demand is measured; do not invent
search volumes or silently import another market's keyword set.

Record the exact product/surface and web-search mode, available model/version,
date/time, language, location/context, exact question, session/history conditions,
answer and displayed source URLs. Save raw observations when permitted. Avoid
personal/client-private data in external prompts. If a product cannot be tested,
mark it untested rather than fabricating a response or counting it as absence.

## Separate observations

| Observation | What establishes it | What it does not establish |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Discovery | A search result returned the URL for the recorded query | Current full-page reading or citation |
| Extraction | Retrieved body contains the tested facts; date/cache and route identified | Consumer product discovery or recommendation |
| Correct answer | Answer matches the confirmed facts and their conditions | That our site was the source |
| Mention | The answer identifies the intended organisation | A link or source citation |
| Citation | Displayed source actually resolves to the recorded site/page | That the whole answer is grounded in that page |
| Referral | Observed visit with supported attribution | A booking/sale or every AI-influenced visit |
| Business result | Reconciled confirmed outcome under the agreed attribution rule | Causation from one SEO/GEO change |

Check where citations resolve, including redirects; distinguish the exact page,
same-domain sources and third-party profiles. Validate a cited passage against
the page rather than assuming that a displayed URL supports every answer claim.
If the page or answer has changed since capture, preserve the dated observation
and state the freshness gap. Record fact disagreements without guessing which
unconfirmed source is true.

## Compare fairly

For a before/after comparison, keep the question set, product/mode, market,
language and session conditions comparable. Preserve the baseline and allow
normal recrawl/cache lag; record intervening site/provider changes. Repeat enough
to expose material variability within the task's time/cost bounds. Do not treat
one favourable response as stable visibility or claim a guaranteed cause.

If summarising citation frequency, show numerator, denominator, date range and
selection rules. For example, 3 cited answers out of 8 completed comparable runs
describes that sample only. Exclude unavailable/failed runs from a visibility
denominator and show them separately; distinguish completed answers without AI
features from answers that could display citations. Do not label a convenience
sample as market share or silently switch to branded prompts after a weak result.

For gaps, compare actually cited pages for the same question. Identify missing
facts, unclear conditions, inaccessible text, contradictory profiles or source
quality. Recommend a specific change and a way to check it. Paragraph formats,
tables and concise answers are testable editorial ideas, not universal ranking
rules. Preserve meaning and business truth rather than copying a competitor.

Use authorised provider reports and existing analytics/booking evidence for
referrals and business outcomes; reuse available Web Measurement expertise.
No referrer does not prove absence of AI influence, and a click is not a booking.
Without access, deliver a runnable observation plan and the supported technical
findings. A fixture run, installed skill or extractor success is not live impact.
