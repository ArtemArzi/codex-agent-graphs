# Grilling

Use a short decision tree to reach shared understanding where an unresolved choice changes the outcome. Read the current request, accepted decisions and the relevant owning documents first. Apply the inherited collaboration and authorization policy.

## Facts and decisions

Find facts in the repository, available tools or relevant primary sources yourself. Delegate a bounded independent investigation only when it repays the handoff; the presence of an unanswered question does not require a subagent.

Ask the user about material choices that the evidence cannot resolve: intended behavior, ownership, business rules, loss tolerance or scope. Choose routine technical mechanisms and test names yourself. Reuse previous authorization and accepted decisions.

## Question rounds

Group the related questions whose prerequisites are already known, normally one to three in an exchange. Number them, explain the practical consequence and give a grounded recommendation. A question depending on an unsettled answer belongs to a later round. Use the host's native question interface when available, otherwise a concise message in the user's language.

While answers are pending, continue independent authorized work. Treat a pending investigation as an unsettled prerequisite; it blocks only the decisions relying on it. Silence and elapsed time are not an answer.

An explicit no-question instruction suppresses ordinary clarification. Record material unknowns or reversible proposed assumptions with their status; do not invent approval for an irreversible or business decision.

## Completion

Record resolved decisions and reasons with their existing owner. Preserve the distinction between accepted requirements, proposals and unresolved questions. Continue the authorized task once its material prerequisites are resolved; a clear task needs no new interview or universal confirmation gate. Leave dependent work pending only where an essential decision or authority remains unavailable.
