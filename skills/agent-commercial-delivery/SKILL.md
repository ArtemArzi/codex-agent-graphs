# Agent commercial delivery

Connect the agent's verified capability to a real customer process, a bounded
offer and an operable handover. Technical readiness, customer acceptance,
payment, repeated use and business effect require different evidence.

Start from the buyer/process owner, actual alternatives, approved behavior,
known integration/access needs and existing commercial decisions. Reuse available
business-work, business-hypothesis-check and value-proposition/go-to-market skills
when those questions matter. Do not rerun generic market research for an already
agreed delivery. Use [pilot-and-offer.md](references/pilot-and-offer.md) to identify
agent-specific unknowns and the smallest useful demonstration or pilot.

Express the offer as the supported process and result, with limits on autonomy,
data, integrations, workload and human fallback. Use evidence for claims about
capabilities, savings or conversion. Interest is a signal, not a confirmed sale.
Do not invent demand, client contacts, accepted prices, business policies or SLA.

## Pilot and acceptance

Select representative user tasks and important failure conditions with the
process owner. Define required evidence and pilot budget before dependent runs.
Use the existing quality owner and available agent-outcome-e2e/native checks.
A polished demo or successful sandbox call does not establish live integration,
reliability, use by employees or economic benefit.

Compare the agent with the current process on comparable work. Account for
model/tool calls, failures, retries, hosting, tracing, human review, support and
onboarding. Report cost per completed process with a defined denominator;
do not present token cost alone as service profitability. Separate measurement
from a causal claim about ROI.

## Handover and operation

Use [handover.md](references/handover.md) to transfer supported behavior, safe
operation, escalation and maintenance. Keep the customer's acceptance and known
exceptions with the existing delivery owner. Route releases through existing
Deployment and later observation through Product Care/native incident processes.

Preserve scope and prior authorization. Preparing an offer grants no authority
to contact prospects, change prices/accounts, sign commitments, activate paid
providers or deploy. Continue authorized preparation without an extra ritual;
leave only the action or consequential decision requiring new authority pending.

Close with the actual state: offer prepared, demo observed, pilot accepted,
transaction recorded, handover completed, adoption observed or effect measured.
Name remaining obligations and evidence gaps. Do not promise that a skill pack
will sell an agent or register future monitoring from a written follow-up note.
