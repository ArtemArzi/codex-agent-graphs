# Landing pages: choose the argument before the composition

Use this profile when a visitor is deciding about a specific offer. A narrow edit
inherits its current page; a working dashboard or CRM does not enter this profile.
Reuse the current task and product brief. Keep one visual owner and the existing
project evidence owner.

## Context and proof

Establish the audience, what brought them here, what they already understand,
the actual offer and the primary next action. Read the real advertisement or
source message when available; do not silently change its promise on the page.
Known facts need no interview. A user who delegates a fictional concept permits
labelled invention, not fabricated evidence about a real business.

For each material claim, identify its source or demonstration and its limits.
Missing prices, availability, guarantees, customer logos, quotes, statistics and
legal conditions remain missing. Use an explicit draft/demo label or omit the
claim. A plausible invented name or a decimal in a number does not make evidence.

## Choose the structure

Explain which visitor question each section answers. Examples are options:

- A high-intent visitor may need a short offer, essential conditions and action.
- An unfamiliar product may need a concrete demonstration and explanation first.
- A service with substantial uncertainty may need process, scope and objections.
- A comparison page needs fair, sourced differences relevant to the decision.

Do not inherit mandatory section counts, three feature cards, three steps,
testimonials, pricing tiers, a FAQ or a tagline animation. Put proof near the
claim it supports. A primary action can repeat; related secondary actions need
appropriate emphasis rather than a blanket ban on useful navigation.

## Choose a visual direction with examples

Use [the visual reference menu](visual-directions.md). Keep a small shortlist of
genuinely different directions when the choice is open. Inspect the relevant
current reference or local gallery; pick one primary world and give any secondary
reference a bounded job. Do not average six styles or force the user through a
tournament. If the user delegates, choose and record the reason yourself.

The menu contains examples, not an approved palette/font list. Read the design
policy and preserve explicit project commitments. Record the chosen typography,
color roles, density and imagery role in the existing brief/DESIGN. Use the
[shared visual contract](visual-contract.md) to carry decisions between tools.

## Content, implementation and outcome

When a specific offer/copy/conversion gap exists, use enabled marketing-skills
modules through their current catalog. Interface Clarity is conditional on a
comprehension gap; neither creates a mandatory multi-skill audit chain. SEO and
indexing decisions retain their existing owner instead of blanket noindex rules.

Write enough actual copy to judge hierarchy, then build in coherent sections.
Preserve the chosen page argument as imagery and spacing change. The CTA leads
to a real supported next step or a clearly identified prototype. Verify its
relevant validation, waiting, failure, success, back and interruption states.
Do not add a skeleton or spinner to an instantaneous operation.

Choose [a product demonstration](product-demonstration.md) only when it helps the
visitor understand. Inspect desktop/mobile and keyboard flow after implementation.
Keep expert observations, behavior tests, human understanding and conversion
measurements distinct. A screenshot or this method cannot establish a conversion
lift. Measured slowness belongs to interface-performance.
