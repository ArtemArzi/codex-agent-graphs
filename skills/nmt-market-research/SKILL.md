# NMT Market Research

Read the supplied brief and relevant authorized project context. Use the user's
language and the known geography. Ask only about an unresolved premise that
would materially change the answer. Acknowledge missing market evidence in the
answer and keep unknown geography, price, or demand explicit.

## Quick discussion

Use this path for a first cut, a few hypotheses or a focused question. Read
only the canon sections needed for that question. Give the requested result
inline on the first substantive turn. Reuse the known product, market, audience
and constraints. An explicit request for a preliminary estimate with the
available inputs calls for an assumption-backed calculation or a statement of
which input prevents it.

- Frame each segment as a buying situation with a shared customer goal and
  success criteria. Treat channel, industry and demographics as attributes
  unless evidence links them to a different goal or criterion.
- State the current alternative, including manual work or doing nothing when
  relevant. Explain the hypothesized improvement in the customer's words.
- Mark what was observed, derived or hypothesized, and give a cheap way to
  disconfirm the most consequential hypothesis. A single source can inform a
  signal to check. Claim a recurring pattern only with the underlying count
  and sources.
- Offer a verdict only when the user asks for a decision. If the idea warrants
  further testing, say **GO (to validation)** and specify what would change that
  recommendation. Use ranges or leave sizing open when evidence is thin.

For example, with a known scheduling product and geography, a quick answer can
compare shops that need to fill same-day cancellations to clinics that need to
reduce avoidable rescheduling with reminders. Give the distinct success
criteria, current workarounds and one check for each. Two ideas require only
the relevant segmentation and Job sections. The discussion stays in chat
unless a saved artifact is authorized.

## Full research

For an explicitly requested full study or Deep report, use the
[full research procedure](references/full-research.md). Use authorized sources
and record what each supports. Cover market sizing, competitors, customer
signals, segment selection, differentiation, ranked strategic options and
field validation. Use the study's evidence floor, source log and self-check.
If a source is unavailable, try an authorized fallback or label the coverage
gap and qualify the affected result. Follow host policy on delegation. The
research can run sequentially whenever a research team is unavailable or
unwarranted.

Lead with a decision summary, followed by the reasoning and detailed evidence.
Show assumptions and ways to check them. Use the existing output owner when
saving a report. Prefer Markdown and honor explicitly requested HTML or inline
delivery.
